How it works

The rule

Each word has to begin with the letters the last word ended on. Those letters are already typed for you, and the word can be just those letters, like me after some.

  • 1 letterlion → night
  • 2 lettersdragon → online
  • 3 letterscomputer → terrain
  • 6 lettersunderground → grounds

Tries and time

You get 20 seconds a turn and 5 tries a word. Every word that is not in the dictionary pops one of the five bubbles, and when they are all gone the match ends. Wrong starting letters and words already used are free.

Phases and depth

Every 50 of streak is a new phase, from 0 to 5 at 250. The water gets deeper, the sound changes, and the matches get longer.

phase 0, streak 0up to 3

The gems

The gems are better than you, so winning is not the goal. Chase a long streak and a high score.

Broken only knows everyday words. It plays short, simple ones and leaves you easy endings, and it loses when it runs out. Your only small chance of winning.

Impure plays everyday words first. When none are left, it finds a rarer word 9 times in 10 and is stuck the other time. Very hard to beat.

Perfect knows every word and picks the ones that leave you the fewest ways to answer. You cannot beat it.

Good to know

Words have to be typed. “Common words only” limits you and the gem to everyday words. Looking up an unfamiliar word online pauses the clock for up to 6 seconds a turn. The gem, the length and common words are locked while a match is running.
