How it works

Each word has to begin with the letters the last word ended on. Those letters are already typed for you, and the word can be just those letters, like me after some.

  • 1 letterlion → night
  • 2 lettersdragon → online
  • 3 letterscomputer → terrain

The water gets deeper as your streak grows, down to the abyss at a streak of 250, and the sound follows it. In escalating mode, past a streak of 50, two and three letter tails become common, and past 150 they take over.

Scoring. Every word is worth 10 points per letter. Words longer than 5 letters earn a growing bonus, with no limit. Matching 2 letters multiplies the word by 1.5 and 3 letters by 2. Each word you play in a row adds 0.05 to a streak multiplier, with no upper limit. The streak resets when a match ends.

Good to know. You get 5 tries per word: every word that is not in the dictionary pops one of the five bubbles, and when they are all gone the match ends. Wrong starting letters and words already used do not cost a bubble. Your opponent is a lapis lazuli gem. A Broken gem plays short, simple words and leaves you easy endings, an Impure gem plays a wider, steadier range of words, and a Perfect gem picks words that leave you the fewest ways to answer. "Common words only" limits you and the gem to everyday words. Looking up an unfamiliar word online pauses the clock for up to 6 seconds a turn.
