How it works

Each word has to begin with the letters the last word ended on. Those letters are already typed for you.

  • 1 letterlion → night
  • 2 lettersdragon → online
  • 3 letterscomputer → terrain

In escalating mode the water gets deeper as the chain grows. Past 100 words, two and three letter tails become common, and past 250 they take over.

Scoring. Every word is worth 10 points per letter. Words longer than 5 letters earn a growing bonus, counted up to 20 letters. Matching 2 letters multiplies the word by 1.5 and 3 letters by 2. Each word you play in a row adds 0.05 to a streak multiplier, up to ×3. The streak resets when a match ends.
